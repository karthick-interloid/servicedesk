> ⚠ **Capture-tooling defect found and fixed during this sync.** Chromium's compositor caps a
> single surface at 16,384px. `page.screenshot({fullPage:true})` past that returns a
> correctly-sized PNG whose lower region is **blank white** — it fails silently. This page is
> ~27,000px tall, so every earlier full-page capture was quietly losing everything below the
> Overlays section. `scripts/capture.mjs` now captures in sub-16,384px windows and stitches them
> with sharp. The earlier section-by-section comparisons used clipped windows and were unaffected,
> but the eight headline PNGs from the first pass were not trustworthy below ~16.3k px.

**Geometry.** Design 26,761px tall at 1440. This build: **27,372px** — +611px / +2.3%, accounted
for by the itemised rows below. Height is **identical at 1440, 1920, 2560 and 3840**: the 1240px
cap holds and content stays centred with no unbounded stretch.
